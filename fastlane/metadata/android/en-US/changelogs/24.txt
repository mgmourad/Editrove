Editrove 0.9.5 — Analysis & Opinion as a newspaper
• Analysis & Opinion is laid out like a real comment page: masthead and dateline, ruled boxes and columns, a lead column with a drop cap, pull-quotes. Short columns run on with the article's own text.
• Sharper photos, without publishers' banners printed over them.
• Audio briefing: a refresh button fetches the latest stories and tells you what changed; it never starts playing by itself.
• Topics: drag to reorder now glides smoothly, one row at a time; "Edit Sources" is no longer cut off.
• Unfolded: cards beside a wide card are as tall as it, so rows end level.
• Settings: proper buttons instead of "(!)" links; every reset asks first, Reset Everything with a clear warning.
• Fix: the Paused banner in Notifications lays out on one line.
