Editrove 0.5.0 — A briefing that sounds like the news
• Audio briefings now play like a broadcast: a dated open, anchor hand-offs between stories, attribution and a sign-off.
• Choose Headlines, Summaries or Full articles, and how many stories (3 to 20).
• Pick the voice and the playback speed (your phone's own text-to-speech voices).
• Four opening cues and a transition sting between stories. The cue starts at full volume, then sits under the narrator and fades away.
• Briefings keep playing in the background and with the screen locked, with lock-screen controls and artwork. Previous and next jump between stories, from the lock screen, headphones or in the app.
• The tab bar now follows your swipe instantly, and the masthead sits clear of the top fade.
